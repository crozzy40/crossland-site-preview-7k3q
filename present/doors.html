The full package

We take a company from top to bottom, one step at a time: the website, the processes, the tools, the training and the way people work. It is a direction toward how companies will work in the future, not a promise of a result.

The standard of the best, fitted to you.

Whatever we build, we start from the best version of it in the industry. We study what the leaders do, then fit it to your business, your people and the way you work, at a fraction of the cost.

How much AI is up to you.

Every level has intelligence built in. You choose how far it goes, and a person approves what matters.

  1. 1

    Smart tool

    A system like the ones on Our work. It watches the numbers, raises alerts, prepares replies and spots patterns on its own. A person decides.

  2. 2

    Assistant

    Something you talk to, like Fred. A voice note or a message, and it keeps the list, the deadlines and the daily brief. Nothing about your money changes without your yes.

  3. 3

    Remote control

    For customers who want that level, the AI can operate software on a computer remotely, within limits you set. We build these as custom packages.

Getting started

Your step: 1 of 5. Your solution: 01 Take a course.

Most of the work runs as before, and AI is still new to the team.

Go to solution 01: Take a course →

Three things to do now

  1. 1Ask one person to try an AI chat on a real task this week, such as a letter or a summary.
  2. 2Agree on one simple rule with your team: customer details and money details do not go into a public AI chat.
  3. 3Start with a short course, so everyone learns the same basics.

Trying it out

Your step: 2 of 5. Your solution: 02 Get trained.

Some people use AI, each in their own way. The work itself has not changed yet.

Go to solution 02: Get trained →

Three things to do now

  1. 1Write down the three tasks your team repeats most each week.
  2. 2Agree in writing with your team what information may go into an AI tool and what may not.
  3. 3Book live training, so the team learns one shared way of working instead of each person their own.

Time for a plan
