The standard of the best, fitted to you.

Whatever we build, we start from the best version of it in the industry. We study what the leaders do, then fit it to your business, your people and the way you work, at a fraction of the cost.

How much AI is up to you.

Every level has intelligence built in. You choose how far it goes, and a person approves what matters.

  1. 1

    Smart tool

    A system like the ones on Our work. It watches the numbers, raises alerts, prepares replies and spots patterns on its own. A person decides.

  2. 2

    Assistant

    Something you talk to, like Fred. A voice note or a message, and it keeps the list, the deadlines and the daily brief. Nothing about your money changes without your yes.

  3. 3

    Remote control

    For customers who want that level, the AI can operate software on a computer remotely, within limits you set. We build these as custom packages.

Where is your company on the AI ladder?

Five steps, in plain words. Find the one that sounds like your company.

Steps 3, 4 and 5 are the three kinds of tool we build: a Smart tool, an Assistant and Remote control. Step 3 starts with a plan.

  1. 1

    We try an AI chat now and then.

    A few people use it on their own, for a letter or an idea. Nothing in the company has changed.

    Your door: Take a course →
  2. 2

    Some of us use it every week.

    It helps with writing and searching. Each person uses it their own way, and the work itself runs as before.

    Your door: Get trained →
  3. 3

    One task runs with AI built in.

    One repetitive job, such as reading invoices or answering the same questions, is done by a tool. A person checks the result.

    Your door: Get a plan →
  4. 4

    AI is part of the daily work.

    Several tasks are connected. The lists, the deadlines and the follow-ups are kept for the team, and people approve what matters.

    Your door: Get it built →
  5. 5

    The routine work runs on its own.

    People handle the exceptions and make the decisions. When something is out of the ordinary, a person is told.

    Your door: Get it built →
