PeoplePeople side

Empowering humans with AI.

What we believe

Much of the work in a company is not the work. It is the paperwork around it: the forms, the checks, the audits and the processes that legal, quality and compliance require. Often it takes more time than the actual work.

We use AI to take that repetitive work off people, so they can spend their time on real tasks: working with customers, developing projects, doing the work they were hired to do. At a normal pace, with time left for life outside work, and more fulfilling days.

That is what "empowering" means to us. It is not only a word. It is why we build what we build.

How we use AI

AI in the way you need it. That is why we say: we are what you need.

We work with the leading AI models, including Claude. We use AI in whatever way suits the customer. There are four ways.

Four ways to run AI
The wayWhere the AI runsWho sees your dataChoose it when
01A local modelOn your own systems.The data stays on your own systems.Your data is sensitive and has to stay with you.
02A large language model, such as ClaudeYou are comfortable using a leading model.
03Hosted by usOn our servers.You want us to run it for you.
04Installed on your serversOn your servers, with the logic of the intelligence kept on ours.The logic stays protected on our side.You want it inside your own walls and the know-how protected.

Whichever way we choose, the aim is the same: take repetitive work off people and leave the decisions with them.

How we use AI ourselves

Built by us.

  • Fred. A brief every morning, and a tracker for a payoff plan, deadlines and papers.
  • The Painting. How we keep track of what the AI tools can do, with the source and date of each fact.
