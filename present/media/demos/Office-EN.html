The office desk

One Monday at a small company that runs on spreadsheets, a shared sheet, invoices sent by email and an accounting system. An agent reads the files, finds what does not match, asks a person and puts the right numbers back everywhere.

Distribuidora La Torcaza: building and hardware supplies, eight people, Colombia. A made-up company.

Six short steps. Read each one, press the amber button, and the result shows right under it. The sample files are at the bottom.

How the numbers move today

  1. Marta's stock sheet → Julián copies it into the orders sheet
  2. Orders sheet → Diana types each order into the invoices file
  3. Invoices file → Diana types them again into the accounting system
  4. Accounting system → checked against Marta's sheet at month end

What the copying costs here

About a person-day a week goes to copying and checking. Mistakes show up at month end, when the numbers do not close.
