What is real

  • You send photos with a line; Nicky crops them, looks at them and writes three captions, alt text and a suggested time.
  • Nothing is posted without your tap. Approval counts only for the version you saw; any change sends it back to draft.
  • Posting goes only through Instagram's official connection. No passwords are asked for or stored.
  • You can also type changes in plain words, such as “shorter” or “move it to Saturday noon”.

What is simulated

  • The photos (drawings), the captions, the time and the post number.
  • The wording of Nicky's messages. The steps are the same as in the real tool.
  • Every name is fictional.
