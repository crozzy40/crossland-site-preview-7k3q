What is real

  • The morning brief at 7:00: the money number, what is overdue, what is coming and your list, in short cards.
  • Voice notes are turned into text and shown back to you before anything happens.
  • To-dos stay on the list until you close them.
  • Money changes are written in plain words by code, and wait for your Yes or No. Nothing about your money changes without your yes.

What is simulated

  • Dana, the amounts, the dates and the weather.
